Top 3 Travel Destinations

Mussoorie

Mussoorie (Garhwali: [məsuːɾiː]) is a hill station and a municipal board in the Dehradun district of the Indian state of Uttarakhand.

It is about 26 kilometres (16 mi) from the state Winter capital Dehradun and 289 km (180 mi) north of the national capital of New Delhi.[4]

Delhi

Delhi,[b] officially the National Capital Territory (NCT) of Delhi, is a megacity and a union territory of India containing New Delhi, the capital of India

There are a number of myths and legends associated with the origin of the name Delhi.

Ayodhya

Ayodhya (Hindi: Ayodhyā, pronounced [ɐˈjoːd̪ʱjɐː] ⓘ) is a city situated on the banks of the Sarayu river[10][11] in the Indian state of Uttar Pradesh

The settlement was historically known as Sāketa until it was renamed to Ayodhya during the Gupta period.[16] The early Buddhist and Jain canonical texts mention that the religious leaders Gautama Buddha and Mahavira visited and lived in the city.
